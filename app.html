Install KeyAudit Pro on your phone

KeyAudit Pro installs straight from your browser — no app store required. It runs full-screen, works offline, and looks and behaves exactly like the iOS and Android apps. Scan keys, check them in and out, and keep the full custody history on the device in your hand.

Already installed? Opening this link launches the app.

iPhone & iPad

  1. 1. Open keyauditpro.com/app in Safari.
  2. 2. Tap the Share button.
  3. 3. Choose Add to Home Screen.

The KeyAudit icon appears on your home screen and opens full-screen.

Android

  1. 1. Open the web app in Chrome.
  2. 2. Tap the menu (⋮).
  3. 3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

You can also tap the install banner inside the app.

Desktop browsers

  1. 1. Open the web app in Chrome or Edge.
  2. 2. Click the install icon in the address bar.
  3. 3. Confirm to install.

Prefer a full desktop install? Download the Windows/macOS app.
