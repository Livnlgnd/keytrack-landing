About the company

Lost keys were never an accident. They were a system problem.

I’m a U.S. Army veteran who spent years in property management before founding Vybelive LLC. In both worlds I learned the same lesson: accountability is not a personality trait, it is a system, and a system only works when it is simple enough to follow on your busiest day. Too often I watched keys go missing and saw good teams blame bad luck, when the real issue was a handoff nobody recorded and a process nobody owned. KeyAudit Pro exists to close that gap — to replace the sticky notes, the memory of the front desk, and the “I thought you had it” with a fast, honest record of every key, every time.

Where it started

Two careers, one lesson

The Army taught me that accountability isn’t a feeling — it’s a process. Someone signs for it, someone returns it, and the record tells the truth. Property management taught me that when that process lives in someone’s memory or a paper log, it breaks the moment things get busy. I spent years watching skilled, well-meaning teams lose track of keys not because they were careless, but because nothing in their day forced the handoff to be captured. The lesson stuck with me: build the record into the routine, and accountability takes care of itself.

The realization

The problem was preventable

Every lost key turned into the same scramble: rekeying units, delayed service calls, angry owners, and a leasing office pointing at each other. None of it was a mystery. It was a missing handoff and a missing record — a completely preventable obstacle.

What I built

A five-second answer to “Who has this key?”

I didn’t want another key cabinet, another subscription, or another cloud account the team would forget to log into. I wanted the simplest possible layer on top of the pegboards and lockboxes people already use. Scan a tag, check a key in or out, and keep an honest history of every handoff. That became KeyAudit Pro.

It runs locally first, because the front desk shouldn’t lose access to its key log when the internet drops. It stays out of your way, because a tool the team won’t use protects nothing. And it assumes people are busy, because the process has to hold up on the worst day, not just the easy one.

Accountability

Every checkout and check-in is recorded with who, what, and when. No more “I thought you had it.”

Simplicity

If it takes more than a scan to use, it won’t get used. The tool has to fit the shift.

Local first

Your data stays on your device. The cloud is optional, not a requirement.

Our mission

Make accountability the easiest part of the job

Vybelive LLC exists to give the people who run properties and dealerships a simple, dependable way to know where every key is and who is responsible for it. Our mission is to make accountability the easiest part of the job — not another chore layered on top of it. We believe a lost key is never really a mystery; it is a missing handoff and a missing record, and both can be fixed without a new key cabinet, a mandatory cloud account, or a week of training.

We build local-first software because the front desk should never lose access to its own key log when the internet drops, and because your operational data belongs to you, not to us. We measure our success by how little time a team spends thinking about our product and how much confidence they gain in their day. Every feature is judged by one question: does it make the honest record faster to create? If it doesn’t, it doesn’t ship.
