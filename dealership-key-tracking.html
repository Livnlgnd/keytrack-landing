Dealership key control FAQ

How do dealerships track car keys?

KeyAudit Pro attaches QR labels to vehicle keys and records each test drive, service loaner, vendor pickup, or lot move with the stock number, VIN, holder, reason, due time, and audit history.

Does KeyAudit Pro replace a dealership DMS?

No. KeyAudit Pro is a physical key custody layer that works alongside the DMS and existing key board.

Can sales and service teams use the same key tracking system?

Yes. Staff can scan keys for test drives, service, vendor custody, and lot moves, while managers review overdue keys and history.
