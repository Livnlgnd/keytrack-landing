Frequently asked questions

Physical key tracking, answered.

Common questions from property management companies and dealership teams evaluating KeyAudit Pro.

What is KeyAudit Pro?

KeyAudit Pro is local-first physical key tracking and audit software. Staff scan QR labels when keys are checked out or returned, creating a timestamped custody record with the holder, reason, due time, and audit history.

Does KeyAudit Pro work offline?

Yes. The core key tracking and custody workflow runs on the local device. Encrypted cloud backup, cloud team visibility, and LAN sync are optional connected features.

Is it for property management companies or car dealerships?

Both. Apartment communities use unit, building, tenant, and vendor language. Dealerships use stock number, VIN, make, model, and vehicle custody language.

How much does KeyAudit Pro cost?

Core licenses start at $500 for up to 100 apartment units or 100 dealership vehicles. Larger apartment tiers are $1,900 for 500 units and $3,150 for 1,000 units. A 14-day free trial supports 50 units or 5 dealership vehicles.

Does it replace our key cabinet or lockbox?

No. KeyAudit Pro is the audit layer for the lockbox, pegboard, or key cabinet you already use. It records who took a key, why, when it is due, and when it returns.

Can managers see key activity across properties and terminals?

Yes. Team invites connect invited terminals with the license and assigned properties already configured. Owners, regional managers, and property managers can view and manage the properties and keys of terminals below them in the invite hierarchy.
